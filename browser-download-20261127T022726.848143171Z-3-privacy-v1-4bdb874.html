Privacy policy

This WhatsApp number runs a personal AI assistant for its owner. It is operated by Buzz Compare LLC. Last updated November 26, 2026.

Who it answers

  • It answers its owner only. WhatsApp messages from anyone else, including group chats, are dropped before any AI model sees them. They are not answered, and their text is not kept.
  • If the owner sends work requests to the assistant's Telegram bot, those messages pass through Telegram, and only his own private messages are taken there. Those requests and the files attached to them are kept on the server with no deletion date.

Other people's content it reads for the owner

  • For the owner, the assistant can read emails sent to his mailboxes, his Google Drive files and calendar, and web pages. These can contain other people's names, words and addresses.
  • Each email it reads is summarized by an AI service; if no summary can be made, the email's subject is stored instead. The summary (or subject) and the sender are kept on the server; with the default setting, a daily cleanup run by the mail service deletes those older than 30 days. If that cleanup does not run, they stay until it next does. The digests he receives carry these summaries, and an urgent email reaches him as an alert with the sender's name and the summary, so both are also in the conversation record.
  • It stores the To and Cc addresses from up to 200 of his sent emails in each mailbox from the last 90 days, with how often he wrote to each, and the senders he marks as important himself, with no deletion date. Removing one only marks it.
  • Text copies of Drive folders he picks for its reference are kept on the server with no deletion date.

Who handles the data

  • Meta: every WhatsApp message and media file passes through Meta's WhatsApp service.
  • Google Cloud hosts the assistant's server and the public web address that receives messages from WhatsApp. That address passes them to the server and does not log what they say. Google Cloud's request logs record each request's web address, time and result, and the caller's internet address and browser type, not its content; the web address of a sign-in return includes a one-time sign-in code.
  • Google: mail is read through Gmail's own interface and files and calendars through Google's, read only.
  • GitHub: the software can read his own code repositories there, read only, for coding work. This connection is not set up yet.
  • Websites: when the assistant visits, signs in to or fills in a website for him, that website receives what it sends, including the password for that site; a weather lookup sends the place to the weather service.
  • AI services: to answer, the assistant sends the owner's messages (text, voice notes, photos, documents and videos), the description of any picture he asks for, and what it reads for him to AI service providers. Its software can use Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, Groq, OpenRouter and DeepSeek; OpenRouter passes each request on to the company that runs the chosen model. Web searches go to Brave Search. Some answers may also be checked by a second AI service from this list.
  • Each provider keeps its own copy under its own terms. Deleting data here does not delete it there.

What is kept, and for how long

  • The conversation. The text of the owner's messages and of the assistant's replies, including voice-note transcripts and video descriptions, is kept on the assistant's server so that he can reply to an earlier message. Whenever a new message is recorded, anything in this record older than 30 days is deleted, and it never holds more than the newest 2,000 messages. For a photo or a document, this record keeps no content; the assistant's reply about it is kept like any reply. Messages he forwards to the assistant (other people's words) are kept in this record like his own.
  • A sign-in code or password he gives through the assistant's sign-in steps is not kept in this record.
  • Message identifiers. Each message's technical identifier and status are kept, with no deletion date, so that a repeated delivery is not answered twice. A message's text is kept with it while it waits for an answer, and is removed from this record once it has been answered.
  • The AI's working copy. The AI software that writes the replies keeps its own record of the conversation on the same server, including what it read from photos, documents, emails, files and web pages (which can include amounts or account numbers). No deletion date is set for it yet.
  • The owner's to-do record. Requests and notes he gives the assistant are kept as numbered items (up to 400 characters of his words each), with his decisions on them (up to 300 characters), what an item is waiting on (up to 200) and the other party's name, number or address (up to 200). There is no deletion date. Closing or dropping an item marks it; it does not erase the words. His reminders are kept in this record too. When the memory feature is on, things he asks it to remember are kept there as well; asking it to forget one marks it and does not erase the words.
  • Files. Photos, documents, videos and voice notes he sends on WhatsApp are read in the server's memory and are not saved as files. Screenshots and PDF copies of websites the assistant visits for him are saved as files and deleted once they are 30 days old, while the browser service is running. When it makes a picture for him, the picture is saved as a file; each time he asks for a picture, pictures older than 30 days are deleted.
  • Passwords and sign-ins. Website passwords he gives the assistant are kept in Google Secret Manager until they are deleted at his request; saving a new one adds a version and does not delete the old one. The sign-in keys for his mailboxes and Google account are kept there too. Each website's browser profile (its cookies, signed-in sessions, browsing history and cached pages) is kept on the server with no deletion date.
  • Other work. Records of tasks he asks for (websites, reminders, building jobs) are kept on the same server. Some are deleted after a day or after 30 days; the rest have no deletion date yet.
  • Sign-ins and limits. When the assistant signs in to a website for him, a record is kept of the time, the site, the task's title and the outcome; the same record notes each password he saves, each password he sent that was not saved in the end, and each sign-in code typed in. Changes to its spending limits are recorded too, with the text of notices it kept instead of sending, the price quotes for top-ups, each override with its stated purpose, and each time sign-ins or device control were switched off or on. No deletion date is set for these records yet.
  • Logs. The server's logs record times, counts and outcomes of what ran, and the names of the websites it connected to or was refused, with the reason it was refused. They are kept as long as the system's default log settings allow; no period has been chosen yet.
  • Backups. Every day, Google Cloud takes a copy of the server's boot disk. It holds everything on this page that is kept on the server: the conversation, the AI's working copy, the to-do record, email summaries, files, browser profiles, task and audit records, and logs. Website passwords he saves and the sign-in keys for his mailboxes and Google account are in Google Secret Manager, not in this copy. The copy does include the server's own service keys (such as those for WhatsApp, Brave Search and the AI services) and the browser profiles' signed-in sessions. Each copy is deleted automatically when it is 30 days old while the server's disk exists; if the disk itself were deleted, the copies then existing would be kept until someone deletes them. While the disk exists, something deleted from the server can remain in copies for about 30 days after it is gone from the server's disk.
  • The owner, and the people he authorizes to maintain the server, can read everything kept on it. Google, as the host, stores it under its cloud terms.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.
