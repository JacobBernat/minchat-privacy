Privacy policy

This WhatsApp number runs a personal AI assistant for its owner, Jacob Bernat (Buzz Compare LLC). Last updated November 20, 2026.

What is processed, and why

  • Messages sent to this number by the owner are processed only to answer the owner.
  • Messages from anyone else are discarded without being read or answered.
  • To produce a reply, the text of the owner's message is sent to the AI model providers the assistant is configured to use: OpenAI, Google and Anthropic.

What is kept

  • The assistant's server deletes the text of a message once it has been answered. It keeps only the message's technical identifier, so that a repeated delivery is not answered twice.
  • Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for any other purpose.
