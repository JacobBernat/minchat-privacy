Privacy Policy — Milan

Last updated November 25, 2026.

Milan is a personal AI agent you talk to on WhatsApp. Milan is operated by Buzz Compare LLC.

What is processed, and why?

Messages sent to Milan's WhatsApp number by the owner are processed only to answer the owner. Messages from anyone else are discarded without being read or answered.

To produce a reply, the text of the owner's message is sent to the AI model providers Milan is configured to use: OpenAI, Google and Anthropic.

Google account data

With the user's explicit consent, Milan accesses Google user data read-only: Gmail (read), Google Drive (read) and Google Calendar (read). Milan uses this data only to answer the user's questions and to prepare digests, briefings and reminders for the user.

Milan's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is kept?

Milan's server deletes the text of a message once it has been answered. It keeps only the message's technical identifier, so that a repeated delivery is not answered twice.

Google account data accessed by Milan is kept only as long as needed to answer the user's questions and to prepare the digests, briefings and reminders the user asked for.

What is never done with your data?

Nothing is sold. Nothing is used for advertising. No human reads your messages or your Google account data except as needed to provide the service at your request.

Deleting data, or questions

You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time. To request deletion of your data, or if you have any questions about this policy, write to support@milan.bot.
